食べる

taberu • [ta-BE-ru] • Head-high / Atamadaka pattern ＼
Mora:
ta
BE
ru
To eat; to consume food

Word Type: Transitive Verb (他動詞 • tadoushi)

lightbulb
Cultural & Usage Nuance

Used for meals and dining. Pair with object particle を (o), e.g., ご飯を食べる (to eat rice/meal). Polite form: 食べます (tabemasu).

format_quote Real-World Context
毎朝、七時にパンを食べます。
まいあさ、しちじに パンを たべます。
Maiasa, shichiji ni pan o tabemasu.
“Every morning at 7 o'clock, I eat bread.”
