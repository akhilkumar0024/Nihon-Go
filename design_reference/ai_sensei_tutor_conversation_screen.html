Sensei, how is 食べる different when speaking with colleagues versus close friends?

Sensei AI 10:43 AM

Great question! Japanese verb levels depend heavily on your social distance (Uchi [内] vs. Soto [外]):

In polite business/colleague settings: Use polite ます form → 食べます (tabemasu).

With close friends/family: Use the dictionary form → 食べる (taberu).

In business meetings referring to clients: You would use Sonkeigo (honorific) → 召し上がる (meshagaru)!

Here is a quick sentence you can repeat aloud:

「一緒にお昼ご飯を食べませんか？」
(Would you like to eat lunch together?)
